- `remove(sku, qty)`: decreases stock. Unknown SKU raises KeyError; removing more than available raises ValueError. When the quantity reaches 0 the item stays in the inventory with qty 0 (it can be restocked later).

- `low_stock()`: sorted list of SKUs whose qty is less than OR EQUAL TO `low_stock_threshold`.
- `total_value()`: sum of qty * price over all items, rounded to 2 decimals.
- `search(term)`: case-insensitive substring match against the item name OR any of its tags; returns items sorted by sku.
- `page(number, size)`: 1-indexed pagination over items sorted by sku; page 1 is the first `size` items; a page past the end returns [].
