Examples:
- merge_intervals([(5,8),(1,3),(2,4),(8,10)]) -> [(1,4),(5,10)]
- find_free_slots([(540,600),(720,780)], 480, 1020, 60) -> [(480,540),(600,720),(780,1020)]
- find_free_slots([(540,600)], 480, 1020, 90) -> [(600,1020)]   because the 60-minute gap 480-540 is too short
- max_concurrent([(0,10),(5,15),(10,20)]) -> 2
